# Demo company: Kite & Kiln

Kite & Kiln is a fictional spatial-commerce startup for Sandora demonstrations.
It helps independent furniture and lifestyle brands turn product catalogs into
immersive 3D storefronts that customers can explore, configure, and share.

The company is intentionally shaped to support two demos:

1. A design-led marketing site with generated 3D assets, image-generation
   experiments, product motion, and a distinct visual taste.
2. A product-management interface with quarterly operating metrics, delivery
   status, risks, and executive reporting.

## Progressive disclosure

Start with `company/PROFILE.md`, then load only the relevant branch:

- `brand/` for visual language, design references, and the owner's taste.
- `product/` for product, users, and roadmap context.
- `projects/website-3d/` for Demo 1.
- `projects/pm-interface/` and `data/quarterly/2026-Q2.md` for Demo 2.
- `people/TEAM.md` only when assigning roles or simulating handoffs.
- `workflows/DEMO_RUNBOOK.md` when running either scripted demo.

All people, companies, metrics, and decisions in this folder are fictional.
