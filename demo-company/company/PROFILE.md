## What the company does

Kite & Kiln gives design-forward brands a way to publish interactive product
rooms without building a 3D platform themselves. A brand imports its catalog,
maps materials and dimensions, and publishes a lightweight web experience with
room scenes, product configuration, and a buy or request-a-quote path.

## Commercial model

- Platform subscription based on active storefronts
- Usage fee for high-resolution scene rendering
- Optional onboarding and asset-conversion package

## Current strategic question

Can Kite & Kiln prove that richer spatial product stories improve qualified
engagement and conversion without making storefronts slow or expensive to
operate?
