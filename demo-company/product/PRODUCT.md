# Product context

## Product

Kite & Kiln Studio is a browser-based workspace for publishing spatial product
experiences.

## Core workflow

1. Import a product catalog and dimensions.
2. Convert approved product photos into optimized 3D assets.
3. Compose a room, collection, or material story.
4. Publish a storefront and share a preview link.
5. Measure qualified views, configuration, inquiry, and purchase intent.

## Primary users

- Brand owner: wants a richer storefront without hiring a 3D engineering team.
- Merchandising lead: controls product stories, materials, and seasonal rooms.
- Spatial designer: creates scenes and asset variants.
- Kite & Kiln operator: watches activation, performance, and support risk.

## Product principles

- Spatial clarity before spectacle
- Fast first render before visual excess
- Every scene has a commercial or editorial purpose
- Data should help a team decide what to improve next
