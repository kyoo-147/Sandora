# Brand brief

## Positioning

Kite & Kiln is the calm technical layer between a beautiful object and the
place where someone imagines living with it.

## Voice

- Specific, warm, and quietly confident
- Short sentences with concrete verbs
- Design-literate without sounding like an agency pitch
- Avoid hype such as seamless, revolutionary, next-gen, and unlock

## Visual direction

- Palette: chalk, kiln black, oxidized copper, mineral blue
- Surfaces: warm paper, dark charcoal, matte metal, occasional translucent
  material studies
- Typography: expressive editorial display face paired with a practical sans
- Geometry: offset columns, generous negative space, considered asymmetry
- Motion: slow object rotation, material reveal, spatial parallax, restrained
  spring interactions
- Density: low on marketing pages, medium on operational product screens

## Taste profile for CEO review

Read `TASTE.md` before a design or frontend agent starts. It records the
Owner's current preferences, dislikes, dial settings, and review questions.
Agents must treat it as a working preference profile, not as immutable brand
truth.
