# Working taste profile

## Current dials

- Design variance: 8/10
- Motion intensity: 7/10
- Visual density: 3/10 for marketing, 6/10 for PM screens

## Positive signals

- Real or generated product imagery with a clear material point of view
- Asymmetric editorial layouts that collapse cleanly on mobile
- Dark charcoal used as a confident field, not as a neon-tech backdrop
- Motion that explains depth, hierarchy, or state
- Subtle grain, tactile surfaces, and strong typography
- Real icon libraries and real screenshots or renders

## Negative signals

- Purple gradients, generic glassmorphism, fake dashboards, and decorative
  crosshair grids
- Three identical feature cards in a row
- Fake precision metrics or invented social proof
- Decorative scrolling labels, status dots, or version stamps
- Motion without a communicative purpose
- Any interaction that ignores reduced motion, keyboard use, or mobile layout

## Review questions

1. What does this visual choice communicate?
2. Can the design still be understood without the animation?
3. Is the product or the craft the hero, rather than the effect?
4. Does the composition have enough contrast and quiet space?
5. Is this a real claim, a sample, or a visual placeholder?
